ABOUT LISA

Preserving moments. Telling stories.

I've always loved taking pictures and preserving the moments that will make you smile years from now.

My loved ones encouraged me to pursue photography, and they continue to celebrate every accomplishment along the way. My incredible husband has been one of my biggest supporters, always encouraging me to keep pursuing my passion. I feel truly blessed.

There is something special about clicking the shutter and seeing what my camera and I capture together. But what makes it even more meaningful is seeing the joy those images bring to the people in them.

I was born on Long Island, New York, and spent many years living in Florida. Seven years ago, I moved to East Alabama to be closer to family, and it has become my home.

I love road trips and discovering new places, so I'm always happy to travel for photography sessions.

Based in East Alabama · Available for Travel
